SLM Foundry

Bring your data. Get a small model that does your job — without an ML team.

Executive summary
One page

Every team is paying frontier-model prices for work a much smaller model could do. SLM Foundry turns your own examples into a specialised model — through a web console, with no training code — and keeps the whole process auditable.

The problem

Most production AI work is narrow and repetitive — classify this ticket, extract these fields, apply our policy. It is routed to a general-purpose frontier model because that is the only option teams have.

  • Cost scales with volume, not with difficulty.
  • Latency makes some features impossible to ship.
  • Data leaves your perimeter on every call.
  • Building the alternative needs an ML team — training code, RLHF plumbing, a labelling tool, evaluation. So it doesn't get built.

The solution

One product, not a toolkit. Upload data, pick a method, launch. Reviewers compare answers in a browser; their judgements become training data automatically. Work runs on shared machines with fair-share scheduling, so many teams use one deployment.
